Base64 Encoder

Use this free online base64 encoder to encode text to Base64. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Base64 Encoder

  1. Paste or type your code or text into the input editor.
  2. Press the action button (Format, Validate, Encode, Decode or Generate).
  3. Copy or download the output. HTML, CSS and JavaScript tools also offer a sandboxed Preview tab.

How it works

Your text is converted to UTF-8 bytes and then encoded with the Base64 alphabet.

Example

"Hello" becomes "SGVsbG8=".

Base64 Encoder FAQ

Is Base64 encryption?

No. It is an encoding that anyone can reverse, so never use it to protect secrets.

Does it support Unicode?

Yes. Text is encoded as UTF-8.

Is the Base64 Encoder free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
