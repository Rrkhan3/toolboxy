BMI Calculator

Use this free online bmi calculator to check your body mass index. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

How to use the BMI Calculator

  1. Enter your values in the fields above.
  2. Press the action button (for example Calculate).
  3. Read the result cards, copy the result, or press Reset to start again.

How it works

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Categories follow common adult ranges: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 normal, 25–29.9 overweight, 30 and above obese.

Example

70 kg at 175 cm gives a BMI of 22.9 (normal weight).

BMI Calculator FAQ

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a general screening number and does not consider muscle, age or body composition. Ask a health professional for personal advice.

Can I use it for children?

No. This tool uses adult ranges and is not suitable for children.

Is the BMI Calculator free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
