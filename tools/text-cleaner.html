Text Cleaner

Use this free online text cleaner to clean up messy text. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Text Cleaner

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. Press the action button. The Word and Character Counters update live as you type.
  3. Copy or download the result, or press Clear to start again.

How it works

Each selected option is applied in turn: remove HTML tags, collapse repeated spaces, trim lines and remove blank lines.

Example

" Hello world " becomes "Hello world".

Text Cleaner FAQ

Is tag removal a security sanitizer?

No. It only removes tag-like text and must not be used as HTML sanitization.

Can I undo a change?

Your original text stays in the input box, so change the options to get a different result.

Is the Text Cleaner free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
