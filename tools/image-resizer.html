Image Resizer

Use this free online image resizer to resize images to any size. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Your image is processed locally in your browser and is never uploaded.

Advertisement

How to use the Image Resizer

  1. Choose an image or drag and drop it onto the upload box.
  2. Set the options you need.
  3. Press the action button, check the preview and download the new image.

How it works

The image is drawn onto a canvas at the new size and exported as PNG, JPG or WebP.

Example

A 4000 × 3000 photo resized to a width of 1200 with the ratio kept becomes 1200 × 900.

Image Resizer FAQ

Will quality be lost?

Shrinking usually looks fine, while enlarging can look blurry.

What does "Keep aspect ratio" do?

It calculates the missing side from the one you enter so the image is not stretched.

Is the Image Resizer free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
