Image Cropper

Use this free online image cropper to crop images to exact pixels. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Your image is processed locally in your browser and is never uploaded.

Advertisement

How to use the Image Cropper

  1. Choose an image or drag and drop it onto the upload box.
  2. Set the options you need.
  3. Press the action button, check the preview and download the new image.

How it works

The selected rectangle is copied from the original image into a new canvas. The top-left corner of the image is position 0, 0.

Example

x 100, y 50, width 400, height 300 crops a 400 × 300 area that starts 100 px from the left and 50 px from the top.

Image Cropper FAQ

How do I know the image size?

If the crop area is outside the image, the error message shows the image dimensions.

Where is the origin?

At the top-left corner of the image.

Is the Image Cropper free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
