QR Generator

Use this free online qr generator to create free QR codes for text, URLs, contacts and more. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

How to use the QR Generator

  1. Choose the type of QR code you need and fill in its details.
  2. Adjust the size, error correction and colors if you like.
  3. Press Generate QR, then download or copy the result.

How it works

You choose a QR type and fill in its details. ToolBoxy builds the correct payload for that type (for example a WIFI: string for Wi-Fi, or a vCard for a contact) and encodes it into a QR code entirely in your browser using a standard, open-source QR algorithm.

Example

Choosing “URL” and entering https://toolboxy.free.nf produces a QR code that opens that page when scanned.

QR Generator FAQ

Do I need an internet connection to generate a QR code?

No. The QR code is created entirely in your browser. An internet connection is only needed to load the page itself.

Can I scan the generated QR code with any phone?

Yes, any modern phone camera or QR scanner app can read it. Codes with very light foreground and background colors may be harder to scan, so ToolBoxy warns you about low contrast.

Is the QR Generator free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
