Remove Duplicate Lines

Use this free online remove duplicate lines to remove duplicate lines from text. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Remove Duplicate Lines

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. Press the action button. The Word and Character Counters update live as you type.
  3. Copy or download the result, or press Clear to start again.

How it works

The first occurrence of each line is kept, repeats are removed and the original order is preserved.

Example

apple, banana, apple becomes apple, banana.

Remove Duplicate Lines FAQ

Is it case-sensitive?

You can choose. Turn off "Case-sensitive" to treat Apple and apple as the same.

Are extra spaces ignored?

Yes, by default leading and trailing spaces are ignored when comparing lines.

Is the Remove Duplicate Lines free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
