Simple Interest Calculator

Use this free online simple interest calculator to calculate simple interest. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

How to use the Simple Interest Calculator

  1. Enter your values in the fields above.
  2. Press the action button (for example Calculate).
  3. Read the result cards, copy the result, or press Reset to start again.

How it works

Simple interest = principal × rate × time ÷ 100. The total amount is the principal plus the interest.

Example

1,000 at 5% for 3 years earns 150 interest, for a total of 1,150.

Simple Interest Calculator FAQ

How is it different from compound interest?

Simple interest is earned only on the original principal. Compound interest is also earned on previously added interest.

Is time entered in years?

Yes. Decimals such as 1.5 years are allowed.

Is the Simple Interest Calculator free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
