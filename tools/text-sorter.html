Text Sorter

Use this free online text sorter to sort lines alphabetically or numerically. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Text Sorter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. Press the action button. The Word and Character Counters update live as you type.
  3. Copy or download the result, or press Clear to start again.

How it works

Text is split into lines and sorted with a locale-aware comparison. Numeric mode sorts by the number at the start of each line.

Example

pear, apple, fig becomes apple, fig, pear.

Text Sorter FAQ

Are blank lines kept?

No. Blank lines are removed before sorting.

Can it sort numbers?

Yes. Choose "Numerically".

Is the Text Sorter free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
