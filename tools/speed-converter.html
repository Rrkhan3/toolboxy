Speed Converter

Use this free online speed converter to convert between speed units. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Speed Converter

  1. Choose the unit to convert from and enter the value.
  2. Choose the unit to convert to and press Convert. Use Swap Units to reverse them.
  3. Copy the result, or press Reset to start again.

How it works

Values are converted to meters per second and then to the target unit.

Example

100 km/h is about 62.14 mph.

Speed Converter FAQ

Which units are supported?

Meters per second, km/h, mph, knots and feet per second.

What is a knot?

One nautical mile per hour, or 1.852 km/h.

Is the Speed Converter free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
