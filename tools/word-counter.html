Word Counter

Use this free online word counter to count words and characters online. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. Press the action button. The Word and Character Counters update live as you type.
  3. Copy or download the result, or press Clear to start again.

How it works

Words are groups of characters separated by whitespace. Sentences are counted by ending punctuation and reading time assumes 200 words per minute.

Example

"Hello brave new world" has 4 words and 21 characters.

Word Counter FAQ

Are spaces counted as characters?

Yes, the main count includes spaces and a separate count excludes them.

How is reading time estimated?

It assumes an average silent-reading speed of 200 words per minute.

Is the Word Counter free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
