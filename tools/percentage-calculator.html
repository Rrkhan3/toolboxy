Percentage Calculator

Use this free online percentage calculator to find percentages and percent change. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

How to use the Percentage Calculator

  1. Enter your values in the fields above.
  2. Press the action button (for example Calculate).
  3. Read the result cards, copy the result, or press Reset to start again.

How it works

Three modes are available: X% of Y = X × Y ÷ 100; X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100; percentage change = (Y − X) ÷ |X| × 100.

Example

20% of 150 is 30.

Percentage Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Choose "% change from X to Y", enter the old value as X and the new value as Y.

Can the result be negative?

Yes. A negative percentage change means the value decreased.

Is the Percentage Calculator free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
